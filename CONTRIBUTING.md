# Collaborating on this research

This is an independent personal research archive. It is not an industrially validated monitoring product or an official institutional publication.

Useful first contributions include:

1. Reproduce the synthetic example and report environment, command, seed and differences.
2. Review one identifiability argument: state assumptions, provide a derivation or counterexample, and distinguish necessary from sufficient conditions.
3. Propose a measurement that would resolve sensor/body orientation or synchronization uncertainty.
4. Propose an evaluation protocol for uncertainty and false alarms across independent recording days.

For an issue or email, use: **question → assumptions → evidence → reproducible steps → expected contribution → limitations**. Link the relevant report section. Mark proposals and synthetic results explicitly. Do not upload raw industrial archives, confidential material or third-party figures without appropriate rights.

Historical findings should remain traceable. Corrections should identify the affected result and preserve its provenance; do not silently replace past outcomes. Software tests are not experimental validation. Report negative outcomes as well as improvements.

This archive currently does not grant a blanket reuse licence. Public visibility and an invitation to discuss do not replace permission to reuse individual materials. Attribution to the original dataset and publication remains necessary. Discuss licensing before substantial redistribution or contributions requiring a new licence.
